Authoritative reference for the LeadSpeed Warranty Claim workflow stages and the role responsible for each stage. Confirmed by John Howard on 2025-06-16 in response to Varun Dabir's role-naming clarification request. *"these look really good varun."*

Use this when a user asks "who handles X step in a warranty claim?" or when configuring user role mapping and access control for warranty work.

## The 11 stages and responsible roles

| # | Stage | Responsible role(s) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Warranty Claim Submission | **Customer**, **Technician**, or **Manufacturer Representative** |
| 2 | Claim Intake & Logging | **Customer Support** / **Warranty Administrator** |
| 3 | Initial Review for Eligibility | **Head of Service** |
| 4 | Field Investigation (if required) | **Field Service Manager** or **Technician** |
| 5 | Root Cause Analysis (RCA) Initiated | Initiated by: **Customer Support**; Conducted by: **Engineering / Quality Control** |
| 6 | Vendor Issue Identified? | **Purchasing Department** |
| 7 | Cost & RMA Tracking | **Warranty Administrator** |
| 8 | Approval Process | **Head of Service** |
| 9 | Claim Resolution – Notify Customer | **Customer Support** |
| 10 | Customer Follow-Up | **Customer Support** / **Service** |
| 11 | Claim Closure & Filing | **Warranty Administrator** |

## Notes for support staff

- These role names are the canonical naming used in the LeadSpeed platform. If a user references a role that does NOT match this list (e.g., asks about "Service Coordinator"), confirm whether they mean one of the above or are using their own org's terminology.
- **Head of Service** has decision authority at two stages (3 — eligibility, 8 — approval). If both reviewers must be different people in your org, that requires a process change, not a config change.
- The submission stage allows three different submitter types — Customer, Technician, or Manufacturer Representative — so the system needs to support self-service from external parties as well as internal users.
