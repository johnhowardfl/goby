## Known failure mode: SSLs.com CNAME validation delay

In May 2026, the `*.bearair.me` PositiveSSL Wildcard had its CNAME record set correctly for domain validation but the cert was **not generated even after CNAME validation completed**. SSLs.com support cited "numerous support requests" causing slower-than-usual response time.

Resolution: Saksoft (Teja) opened ticket `SSL-NRR-4623` with SSLs.com on 2026-05-08. Cert was generated by 2026-05-11. Total delay from CNAME validation to cert issuance: ~3 days.

If this recurs: open a support ticket at SSLs.com immediately after CNAME validates, don't assume automated processing will work.
